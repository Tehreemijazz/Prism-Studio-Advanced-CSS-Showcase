Questions, answered

Built with native <details>, so it works by keyboard out of the box.

Do the effects hurt performance?
Motion sticks to transform and opacity, blur is used sparingly, and will-change appears only on tilting cards.
What if a browser lacks scroll-driven animations?
@supports gates the CSS version; a small IntersectionObserver and scroll listener provide the fallback.
Is it accessible?
Focus states are visible, everything is keyboard reachable, and prefers-reduced-motion switches movement off.
